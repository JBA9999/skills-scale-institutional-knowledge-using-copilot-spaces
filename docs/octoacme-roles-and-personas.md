# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance and acceptance validation. They define test strategies, coordinate testing activities, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans aligned with feature acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before PR approval
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Conduct smoke tests and regression testing before releases
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Escalate quality risks and blockers to PM and Tech Lead

### Goals
- Ensure customer-facing quality and reliability
- Catch defects early to reduce production incidents
- Enable fast, confident releases through thorough testing

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria review during sprint planning
- Quality status in weekly delivery syncs
- Defect reports and test reports

---

## Security Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure projects meet security standards, manage security risks, and coordinate incident response. They provide security expertise and governance across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and design for security risks
- Oversee security scanning in CI/CD pipeline
- Respond to and triage security incidents
- Coordinate security incident communications and post-mortems
- Provide security best practices guidance to development teams
- Maintain security compliance documentation
- Escalate critical security issues following incident runbook

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities in production
- Ensure incident response speed and transparency
- Maintain compliance with security standards and regulations

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and code review
- Security incident alerts and triage coordination
- Post-incident blameless retrospectives
- Security risk entries in risk register

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business value, decision authority, and customer needs. They provide context, approve key decisions, and ensure projects remain aligned with strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and major scope changes
- Participate in kickoff and planning meetings
- Receive and review weekly/monthly status updates
- Make priority and trade-off decisions
- Escalate blockers and risks affecting delivery
- Communicate outcomes to broader organization

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivered
- Ensure alignment with strategic initiatives
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and transparency

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and planning meetings
- Weekly status updates and risk escalations
- Monthly stakeholder briefings
- Decision meetings on scope or priority changes

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical strategy, architecture decisions, and quality standards. They guide developers, review designs, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Review technical designs and PRs for quality and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on best practices and coding standards
- Make trade-off decisions on technical approach
- Coordinate with other Technical Leads on dependencies
- Ensure test coverage and code quality standards are met

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable team velocity through clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- PR reviews with technical feedback
- Technical risk escalation in planning and weekly syncs
- Guidance on testing strategy and quality standards
